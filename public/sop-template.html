Worked example — the quality bar

Example SOP
Hook must echo the avatar's own words
other
copywriter
(blank — global)
Every hook must reuse phrasing pulled from the avatar's real verbatims — never generic marketing language.
  1. Open with the wound (mirror / photo / closet moment), then pivot to the desire within the first 3–5 seconds.
  2. Reach for the avatar's power words; ban the rejected clichés ("bounce back", "snap back", "get your body back", "transformation").
  3. Match her sentence rhythm — flat declaratives, ellipsis on the hardest feeling, ALL-CAPS on the one weight-bearing word.
  4. Never claim a cure. Compression supports; it does not fix, cure, or eliminate.
